Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your appointment.

⏳ How long do lash extensions last?
  • Lash extensions typically last 2–4 weeks depending on your natural lash cycle and aftercare.
  • We recommend infill appointments every 2–3 weeks to keep your lashes looking full and fresh.
  • With proper aftercare, your extensions can last closer to the 4-week mark before a full set is needed.
🩺 Allergy & Safety Information
  • By booking with Puppy Beauty Bar you confirm you have no known allergies to products used, and you have disclosed any relevant medical conditions to the studio.
  • Puppy Beauty Bar accepts no liability for reactions arising from undisclosed allergies or medical conditions.
✨ What's the difference between Classic, Hybrid, and Volume lashes?
  • Classic: One extension per natural lash — ideal for a natural, defined look.
  • Hybrid: A mix of classic and volume fans — perfect for subtle fullness with a natural base.
  • Volume: Multiple ultra-fine extensions fanned onto each lash — creates a dramatic, glamorous finish.
  • Not sure which is right for you? Mention your ideal look in your booking notes and we'll advise.
🛁 How do I prepare for my appointment?
  • Wash your hair and shower before your appointment — you'll need to avoid water for 12 hours afterwards.
  • Arrive with a clean, makeup-free face — no eye makeup, foundation, or mascara.
  • Wearing contact lenses is recommended to reduce eye sensitivity during the treatment.
  • Avoid caffeine and stimulants beforehand if you find it hard to keep your eyes still for long periods.
💧 What aftercare do I need to follow?
  • Avoid water, steam, and sweat for the first 12 hours after your appointment.
  • Use only oil-free skincare and makeup around the eye area — oil breaks down the adhesive.
  • Brush your lashes gently with a clean spoolie each morning.
  • Avoid rubbing your eyes or sleeping face-down. A silk pillowcase helps prolong your set.
  • We sell professional aftercare products in studio to help you maintain your lashes at home.
💄 Can I wear makeup with lash extensions?
  • Yes — but use oil-free products only. Oil-based makeup will dissolve the lash adhesive.
  • Avoid waterproof mascara, which is very difficult to remove without damaging extensions.
  • If you do wear mascara, apply it only to the tips (not roots) and use a water-based formula.
  • Use an oil-free makeup remover and be gentle around the eye area when cleansing.
🔒 Are lash extensions safe?
  • Yes — when applied by a trained technician using professional-grade materials, lash extensions are safe.
  • We use only skin-safe adhesives and premium lash materials, sanitised between every client.
  • Please disclose any eye conditions, recent surgery, or medical procedures before your appointment.
  • If you have known allergies or sensitivities, please let us know before treatment so we can advise appropriately.
❓ How do I book, and what is the deposit for?
  • Book online using the Book Now form — choose your service, date, and time.
  • A £10 deposit is required to secure your appointment. It is deducted from your final balance.
  • The deposit is non-refundable for late cancellations (less than 24 hours) or no-shows.
  • You'll receive a confirmation within 24 hours. For any questions, contact us directly.
